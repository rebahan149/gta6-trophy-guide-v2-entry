    The road to the platinum

    Eight stages, in the order to play them, built from the official trophy descriptions: the chapter and named-mission trophies fix the story order, the stranger finales form their own block, and the counters and collectibles fill the stages after the finale. The official list holds no online trophies — every step below is single-player. Each stage carries its own cited notes, and trophies flagged Missable are the ones whose descriptions tie them to a moment or a choice that may not come twice; everything else can be cleaned up in free roam.

    Missables: the five to plan around

    These flags are derived from the official descriptions — a trophy is flagged when its description ties it to a single story moment, a choice, or a scripted first attempt. Each card quotes the official description, states the basis for the flag as its own cited claim, and gives the trophy's cited steps, so you can see exactly when the moment comes and how to protect it. A manual save before story decisions is the cheapest insurance in the game.

    Sources & method

    The trophy names, descriptions and grades in this guide are the official PlayStation 5 list for Grand Theft Auto VI, as published by PlayStation and mirrored by the two trophy databases below before this guide was written; both mirrors agree on every name, description and grade. Every claim in every guide names its sources beside it — the chips under each step are those sources — and the counts in the header are computed from the guide's own data file, not typed in. The roadmap order and the missable flags are this guide's own work, derived from the official descriptions; the reasoning is stated as a cited claim on each flagged trophy. Links open only when you follow them; the app itself fetches nothing and works with the network off.
